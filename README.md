## 🌌 About the Project

ASTROCARE is an innovative digital health monitoring platform developed by **Team Retro-V** for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

Human health is one of the greatest challenges of long-duration space exploration. Astronauts experience unique physiological changes due to microgravity, radiation exposure, isolation, and other environmental conditions.

ASTROCARE aims to make health-related information more accessible and understandable through an interactive digital platform that brings together health indicators, data visualization, and human body system representations.

Our goal is to demonstrate how data-driven technologies can contribute to understanding, monitoring, and supporting human health in space.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Develop an interactive platform for visualizing human health information.
- Present important physiological indicators through understandable dashboards.
- Explore the effects of space-related environmental conditions on human health.
- Utilize scientific datasets to support meaningful health-related visualizations.
- Make complex health information easier to interpret through interactive interfaces.
- Demonstrate how digital healthcare technologies can support future space exploration.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧬 1. Interactive Human Anatomy
Explore human body systems through visual representations of human anatomy, helping users understand the different physiological systems.

### 📊 2. Health Monitoring Dashboard
A centralized dashboard designed to present health-related indicators and provide an accessible overview of physiological information.

### 📈 3. Data Visualization
Interactive graphs and visual elements help transform complex datasets into understandable information and highlight relevant health patterns.

### 🛰️ 4. Space Health Awareness
Explore health challenges associated with the space environment and understand why astronaut health monitoring is essential for long-duration missions.

### 💡 5. User-Friendly Interface
A modern and intuitive interface designed to make health information accessible, engaging, and easy to navigate.

## 🧪 Data & Scientific Foundation

ASTROCARE incorporates scientific and health-related datasets to support its data visualization and monitoring concepts.

We aim to use reliable data sources to demonstrate how physiological information can be interpreted and presented in an accessible way.

Potential data categories include:

- Human physiological measurements
- Cardiovascular health indicators
- Respiratory system parameters
- Human body and anatomical information
- Spaceflight-related health research

**Data integrity:** Dataset-derived information should be distinguished from illustrative or simulated values. ASTROCARE is a prototype and does not replace professional medical assessment or provide a clinically validated diagnosis.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **Frontend:** Web-based interactive user interface
- **Data Visualization:** Interactive charts and graphical representations
