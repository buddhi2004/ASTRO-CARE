🧠

1. Zero-Cloud Local Inference

All clinical decisions, SANS optic nerve sheath diameter (ONSD) evaluations, and jugular vein thrombosis screenings run directly on device via high-fidelity JSON rule-engines with 0 ms latency.

HUMAN RESILIENCE & EARTH IMPACT

Autonomous Crew Health for Deep Space & Remote Earth

Long-duration spaceflight requires medical systems that operate without real-time ground support—creating a blueprint for offline-first healthcare in both deep space and underserved communities on Earth.

🚀

Deep-Space Medical Autonomy

When crews travel to Mars, communication delays reach up to 20 minutes each way. ASTRAL-MED eliminates reliance on live Earth connectivity by running 100% offline triage, guided ultrasound protocols, and space-weather risk adjustments directly on the crew's local device.

• Zero-cloud local JSON rule-engine for instant DVT & SANS triage
• NASA DONKI solar flare & radiation alerts triggering proactive hydration checks
🌍

Earth Impact & Long-Term Recovery

The same offline-first guidance built for non-doctor astronauts translates directly to rural clinics, disaster zones, and post-flight rehabilitation—ensuring both astronauts returning from microgravity and patients on Earth recover safely.

• 45-day post-landing physiotherapy & reconditioning plan (Mode B)
• Plain-language self-exam guides adaptable for remote community health workers
